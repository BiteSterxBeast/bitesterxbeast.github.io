Five possible shapes

Hover or tap a bar for that hour's numbers.
These are guesses, not data. YouTube doesn't publish hourly views retroactively, so nothing here is measured — it's five statistically plausible ways your view gain could have been spread across the window. The totals are exact; the hour-by-hour distribution is invented. Use it to picture the shape, never to report real numbers.

Draw the graph you saw

Drag across the box to trace the shape of the graph from your analytics — height is relative, so don't worry about exact numbers. The tool scales whatever you draw so the hours still add up to your real view gain, then builds variations around it. Anything you leave untouched gets filled in by joining the nearest points you did draw.
