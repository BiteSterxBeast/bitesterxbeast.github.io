Five possible shapes

Hover for an hour's numbers. Click to magnify (3 steps), right-click to zoom back out.
These are guesses, not data. YouTube doesn't publish hourly views retroactively, so nothing here is measured — it's five statistically plausible ways your view gain could have been spread across the window. The totals are exact; the hour-by-hour distribution is invented. Use it to picture the shape, never to report real numbers.

Draw the graph you saw

Trace the shape of the graph from your analytics — one bar per day by default, so you sketch the day-to-day trend and the tool fills in each day’s hourly rise and fall for you. Hit Trace Image to load a screenshot and draw straight over the top of it — height is relative, so don't worry about exact numbers. The tool scales whatever you draw so the hours still add up to your real view gain, then builds variations around it. Anything you leave untouched gets filled in by joining the nearest points you did draw.

Per-day view counts

If you know how many views a video picked up on individual days, enter them here and each of those days gets locked to that exact number — the hours inside it still get guessed, but the daily totals will be right. Leave a day blank and it shares whatever's left over from the window. Your start and end view counts don't change.
